What we do

Help communities scarred by wars.

We provide housing, food, water, and other resources to places that wars have destroyed

Take in refugees and take care of them.

We provide housing, food, water, and other resources to those who had to leave their homes because of reasons outside of their control.

Give aid after natural disasters.

We provide housing, food, water, medical care, and other resources to those who need it after a natural disaster.

Give medical care to people who can't get it anymore.

We provide medical care to communities that, through war, disasters, or other reasons, have lost access to medical care.
